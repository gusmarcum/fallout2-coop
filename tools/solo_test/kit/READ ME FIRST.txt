SOLO TEST: party experience, tested by one person
=================================================

What this is
------------
A separate copy of the game with its own test server. Nothing here touches your
real worlds or their saves, and nothing you do in the test is kept: every start
puts the same save back.

One PC can run only one game client, so you cannot open a second game for the
second player. A small script holds their seat instead. It does not play. It
stays connected so the game counts two players, and its window tells you what
the second player's screen is being sent and what every award paid to whom.


How to run it
-------------
  1. Close any other Fallout 2 that is running (Steam's, or another co-op game).
  2. Double-click  "1 START TEST.cmd"   and leave that window open.
     Wait for the line:  Seated as <your friend> (seat 1).
  3. Double-click  "2 JOIN.cmd".   You are in the game as your own character,
     in a window, so you can see the first window next to it.
     The first window says:  The host joined. Both players are in the game.


What to try, and what you should see
------------------------------------
Keep an eye on two things: YOUR message log in the game, and the first window.

  A quest award        Double-click "3 Quest monster.cmd".
                       The Klamath rat god appears right next to you, with his
                       real script, and attacks. Kill him.
                         your log:       the script's line, then
                                         "You gain 300 experience points."   one time
                         when the fight ends:
                                         "... you earn 150 exp. points."     one time
                         first window:   two AWARD blocks, each listing both
                                         characters with the SAME amount and
                                         "paid once each to 2 players"

  A kill               Double-click "4 Two raiders.cmd".
                       Two raiders appear next to you and do nothing. Attack one,
                       kill both, then END COMBAT yourself (the END CMBT button):
                       a fight you start among bystanders does not end by itself,
                       in the normal game either.
                         your log:       "... you earn 260 exp. points."     one time
                         first window:   one AWARD block, 260 to both

  A skill              Get hurt in one of the fights, then use First Aid on
                       yourself. It can fail; try again if it does.
                         your log:       "You earn 25 XP for honing your skills."
                         first window:   your friend's screen shows
                                         "You earn 25 XP from <you> honing a skill."
                                         and one AWARD block, 25 to both

  A plain reward       Double-click "5 Quest reward.cmd".
                       500 experience, paid the way play pays it. An operator
                       award prints nothing in the game's log; open your
                       character screen and the 500 is there, one time.

  The numbers          "6 Character sheets.cmd" shows both characters' level and
                       experience whenever you want to compare.
                       Your own character screen in the game must agree with it.

  Another round        "7 Heal everyone.cmd" heals both characters.


What counts as a pass
---------------------
  - Every award shows up ONE time in your message log, not two.
  - Your experience on the character screen goes up by exactly the amount in
    the line you read.
  - Every AWARD block in the first window lists both characters with the same
    amount and ends with "paid once each to 2 players".

What counts as a failure
------------------------
  - The same award twice in your log, or your experience going up by double.
  - An AWARD block that lists only you while the first window says your friend
    is seated.
  - The words "SOMEBODY IS ON THIS AWARD TWICE" in the first window.
If you see any of these, copy the text of the first window and the file
server-console.log from this folder.


Ending the test
---------------
Quit the game, then close the first window (or double-click "9 STOP TEST.cmd").
The test server stops by itself when the last player leaves.


What is in this folder
----------------------
  test-settings.cmd    the two character names, the save slot and the ports
  test-save\           the save every test starts from
  tools\               the stand-in and the helper the launchers call
  server-console.log   what the test server printed during the last run
Ports 9800 and 9801 are used, on this PC only. Your real worlds use other ports
and can stay as they are, but only one game client can run at a time.
